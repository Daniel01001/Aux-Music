Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Aux is a social music app run by Mukelwa Hlatshwayo in Johannesburg, South Africa ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account ID (stored scrambled with a secret key), your name, and a link to your Google profile photo, which we only show if you turn it on. Google also sends your email address; we don't keep it. See Your data & security.
  • What you create: your @ID, display name, bio, mood and mood song, and any profile photo you upload; your playlists; songs you add, like or save; parties and syncs you start or join, and what you say in party chat.
  • Credits: a record of every credit you earn or spend, and which songs you've earned listening credits for.
  • Devices: for each device you sign in on, a short label (for example "Chrome on Android") and when it was last active. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of your sign-in token.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the push address your browser gives us, and the notes in your inbox.

We don't collect your location, contacts or payment details, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your playlists, let friends find you by your @ID, keep credits fair, and sync playback.
  • To tell you when someone adds a song to your playlist or likes a song you added.
  • To keep the service safe and fix problems.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts Aux and stores its data.
  • Google handles sign-in. Aux uses YouTube API Services: YouTube songs play in YouTube's own player, and searches go to YouTube. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Audius and Jamendo stream songs from independent artists. When you play one, your device connects to their servers.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla) delivers notifications. We send it no message content.
  • LRCLIB supplies lyrics. Our server asks it by song title, artist and length only, never anything about you.

We don't sell your information. Other Aux users can see your @ID, name, photo (if shown), bio, playlists, and the songs you add and like. If you start a public party, its name and your @ID are listed for everyone, and anyone with the link can join and read the party chat.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, playlists, the songs you added to other people's playlists, your likes, credits, inbox, devices and notification settings straight away. Server logs may hold traces for up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: You → Download my data.
  • Correct it: You → Edit.
  • Delete it: You → Delete my account.
  • Sign out other devices and turn notifications off on the You page.

You can also object to how we use your information, or complain to South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Children

Aux is not for children under 13.
