Your data & security

Plain answers to the questions people ask about signing in with Google. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Does Aux see my Google password?

No, never. You type your password on Google's own page, not in Aux. Google then tells Aux "this is the same person as before", and that's all.

What can Aux do with my Google account?

Nothing beyond signing you in. Aux asks Google for the most basic sign-in only. It has no access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, calendar, YouTube account, history or payments, and it can't post anything as you.

What does Aux keep?

  • A scrambled sign-in ID. Google gives every app a number for your account. Aux stores it scrambled with a secret key, so a copy of our database alone can't be linked to your Google account.
  • Your name and the @ID you chose. You can change both.
  • Not your email address. Google sends it, but Aux doesn't keep it. We deleted the ones collected before 1 November 2026.
  • Your Google photo only if you turn it on. It's off unless you choose "Use my Google photo".
  • What you do in the app: playlists, songs you add or like, your mood, credits, and party chat. The privacy policy has the full list.

Is it encrypted?

  • Everything you send and receive is encrypted with HTTPS, the same protection banks use. The app refuses unencrypted connections and tells your browser to always use the secure address.
  • Your sign-in is never stored readable. Each device gets its own sign-in key, and our database keeps only a scrambled (hashed) fingerprint of it, which can't be turned back into the key.
  • Photos you upload are encrypted when stored by our host, Cloudflare.
  • Party chat is not end-to-end encrypted. It travels encrypted but the Aux server can read it, like most group chats. Don't share anything private in a party.

What else protects me?

  • The app only lets its own code, Google sign-in and YouTube's player run on its pages. This blocks a common way of stealing sign-ins.
  • No adverts and no tracking companies.
  • Under You → Devices you can see everywhere you're signed in and sign any device out.

How do I take back control?

  • Download everything Aux holds about you: You → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: You → Delete my account. It's removed straight away.
  • Disconnect Aux from Google: at myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Aux, then "Delete all connections". Do this after deleting your Aux account.
